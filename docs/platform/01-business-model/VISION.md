## What Problem Does LocalPlug Solve?

Premium airport transfers and tourism concierge in Medellín, Colombia are fragmented.

Travelers arrive at José María Córdoba Airport and face:
- No reliable way to book a premium transfer
- No visibility into driver quality or vehicle condition
- No coordination between airport pickup, hotel check-in, and tourism experiences
- Language barriers with local operators
- No single point of contact for their entire trip

Drivers face:
- No efficient way to receive premium bookings
- No compliance tracking or performance visibility
- No direct connection to quality-conscious travelers

Dispatchers face:
- Manual matching of drivers to bookings
- No real-time visibility into fleet status
- Scattered communication across WhatsApp, phone, and email

**LocalPlug solves this by being the single platform that connects all three.**

---

## The Vision

**LocalPlug is the Business Operating System for premium airport transfers and tourism concierge.**

It is not a booking app. It is not a driver management tool. It is the platform that
every application — Admin, Driver, Customer, Hotel, Partner, API — consumes to deliver
premium ground transportation and tourism services.

Like Shopify didn't start with POS, App Store, and Checkout — they started with the Core.
LocalPlug started with the Core. Now every new application is a consumer of that Core.

## Where We're Going

**Phase 1 (Now):** Core Platform — domains, events, infrastructure
**Phase 2 (Next):** Applications — Admin, Driver, Customer, Landing
**Phase 3 (Future):** Ecosystem — Hotel Portal, Partner Portal, API, SDK
**Phase 4 (Vision):** Multi-city — Medellín → Bogotá → Cartagena → LATAM

The platform is city-agnostic by design. The business model is city-specific.
That's the key insight that makes scaling possible.
