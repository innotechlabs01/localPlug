# BUSINESS MODEL (01-business-model)

> **The stable core that guides everything.**
> This layer is the MOST STABLE part of the documentation.
> It answers: WHAT is LocalPlug and WHY does it exist.
> Everything else (architecture, engineering, apps) serves this.

---

| `CAPABILITIES.md` | What the platform can do | Granite |

## The Rule

**Business Model never changes because of a technical decision.**
Technical decisions serve the business model, not the other way around.

If a technical decision requires changing the business model, stop.
That means the architecture is guiding the business — wrong direction.
